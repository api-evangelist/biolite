Get The Gang Pumped To Get Outside With This Custom Sticker Pack The marketing team here at BioLite felt that the available campsite emojis were lacking important feelings that can only be felt outside or on the trail. So, we compiled 20 of our favorite moments, feelings, and friends and turned them into a custom set of emojis. We are proud to introduce our very own sticker pack of #CampMojis that can be downloaded for free from the App Store on iOS and Android.
