BioLite’s new home energy appliance is recognized for outstanding design and global impact Brooklyn, NY (December 19, 2024) — BioLite , a social enterprise leader in resilient off-grid energy solutions, is proud to announce that its recently launched home energy appliance, Backup by BioLite , has received one of the most coveted recognitions in technology as a CES Innovation Awards® 2025 Honoree . The announcement comes ahead of CES 2025, the world’s most powerful technology event, happening Jan. 7-10 in Las Vegas, NV.
